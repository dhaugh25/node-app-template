Frequently Asked Questions

How do I solve differential equations?

Check out the Differential Equations tutorials online. You can also visit the Math Lab for one-on-one help.

How do I balance chemical equations?

Start by listing the number of atoms for each element on both sides of the equation. Balance elements that appear in only one reactant and one product first. Leave hydrogen and oxygen for last since they often appear in multiple compounds. Adjust coefficients—not subscripts—until both sides have the same number of each atom.

Where can I get coding help for CS classes?

Check your course's GitHub, Discord, or Canvas pages for peer coding sessions. Many professors also host office hours or Piazza threads specifically for coding questions.

How do I cite sources for lab reports?

Use MLA, APA, or IEEE depending on your class guidelines. Check the MLA guide or APA guide.

How do I approach engineering research?

Check out the Engineering Research page for guidance on research projects, faculty labs, and student opportunities.

How do I prepare for lab experiments?

Review your lab manual, pre-read experiment instructions, and consult your TA or lab instructor with any questions before the session. For extra help, ask peers or visit the relevant help center for your discipline.

How can I get involved in STEM student organizations?

Look for student clubs and organizations on ASU Clubs Directory or check with your college's student services office. Many groups offer tutoring, workshops, and peer mentoring in STEM fields.
